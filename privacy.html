This policy explains what information Gleam collects when you book a detail or apply as a detailer, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Gleam is an online marketplace that connects people who want their car detailed with independent detailers who come to them. This site is run by Gleam. In this policy, “Gleam”, “we” and “us” mean Gleam.

Questions about your information? Email [your business email].

What we collect

When you request a detail

  • Your name, phone number and, if you give it, your email address
  • The street address and ZIP code where your car will be
  • The package, add-ons, price, preferred time and any notes you add
  • The detailer you chose

When you apply as a detailer

  • Your name or business name, email, phone number and street address
  • Your ZIP code, city, state and how far you’ll travel
  • The packages and add-ons you offer, your prices and a short description of your work

Automatically, when you use the site

  • Pages you visit, the device and browser you use, and roughly where you’re visiting from, collected by our website platform for analytics, if you allow analytics (see Cookies and similar storage)
  • Small items saved in your browser: the ZIP code you searched, a visitor session token that lets the site talk to our booking system, and your cookie choice

We don’t collect payment card details on this site.

How we use it

  • To send your booking request to the detailer you chose, so they can confirm a time with you
  • To contact you about your request or application
  • To review detailer applications and show approved detailers to customers nearby
  • To run, secure and improve the site, including blocking spam and fraud
  • To meet legal obligations and resolve disputes

Who we share it with

  • The detailer you choose. When you send a request, that detailer receives your name, phone, email (if given), the car’s address, your preferred time, notes and what you booked.
  • Customers, for approved detailers. If your application is approved, your name or business name, city and state, ZIP code, travel radius, prices, add-ons and description are shown publicly so customers can find you. Your email, phone and street address are not shown.
  • Service providers who run the site for us: Wix.com (hosting, database and analytics), Google (web fonts, and Google Analytics only if we turn it on and you allow analytics) and jsDelivr (delivers code and ZIP code data to your browser). They may only use the information to provide their services.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of Gleam, our users or others.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t share it for targeted advertising.

Cookies and similar storage

We use two kinds:

  • Essential: needed for the site to work, such as remembering your ZIP code and keeping a visitor session for sending requests. These are always on.
  • Analytics: help us understand which pages people use, through our website platform’s analytics (and Google Analytics if we turn it on). These run only according to your choice.

You can change your choice at any time with , which is also linked at the bottom of every page. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a choice of essential only.

How long we keep it

We keep booking requests and detailer applications for as long as we need them to provide the service and handle any follow-up, and generally no longer than 24 months after your last activity, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. Approved detailer listings stay up until the detailer asks us to remove them or we remove them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we have about you, or to stop contacting you. Email [your business email] and tell us what you’d like. We may need to confirm it’s you before we act, and we’ll respond within 45 days.

Depending on where you live, state privacy laws may give you additional rights. We’ll honor them where they apply, and we won’t treat you differently for using them.

Security

The site uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Booking requests and applications are stored in a database that only Gleam’s administrators can read; other visitors can’t see them. No system is perfectly secure, so please don’t put sensitive information, such as passwords or payment details, in the notes field.

Children

Gleam is for adults. It isn’t meant for anyone under 18, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we’ll make that clear on the site.
