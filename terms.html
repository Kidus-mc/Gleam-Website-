What Gleam is

Gleam is a marketplace. We help customers find independent car detailers who travel to them, and we help detailers find customers.

  • Gleam doesn’t detail cars. The detailing is done by the detailer you choose.
  • Detailers are independent businesses, not Gleam employees or agents. Each detailer sets their own prices, decides which services and areas they cover, and is responsible for their work.
  • We review detailer applications before listing them, but we can’t guarantee the quality, timing or outcome of any detailer’s work.

Booking requests and pricing

  • Sending a booking request doesn’t charge you and isn’t a confirmed booking. Your detailer or Gleam will contact you to confirm a time.
  • Prices are set by each detailer and shown before you send a request.
  • A 7% Gleam service fee is added to the detailer’s price. The total, including the fee, is shown before you send your request.
  • You’ll be told how to pay when your booking is confirmed. The final price can change only if you and the detailer agree to change the work, for example adding an add-on on the day.
  • If you need to cancel or reschedule, tell your detailer as early as you can. Any cancellation terms will be confirmed with you when your booking is confirmed.

If you book a detail

  • Give accurate contact details and the correct address for the car.
  • Make sure you have permission for the detailer to work on the car at that location, and that the car is accessible at the agreed time.
  • Remove valuables from the car before the detail.
  • Treat detailers with respect. We may refuse service to anyone who doesn’t.

If you’re a detailer

  • Give accurate information about yourself, your services and your prices, and keep it up to date.
  • Hold any licenses, permits and insurance that the law requires for your work, and follow local rules, including those on water runoff and working on private property.
  • Honor the prices you list, and do the work you agree to do with reasonable care and skill.
  • Use customer information only to provide the service they requested.
  • Gleam may approve or decline applications, and remove or pause listings, at its discretion, for example after customer complaints.
